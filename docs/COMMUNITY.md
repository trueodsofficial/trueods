### Join the support group

1. Email your Fab proof of purchase to [trueodssupport@gmail.com](mailto:trueodssupport@gmail.com) with the subject **[TrueODS Telegram] Join request**.
2. Include the order number, purchased product name and proof of purchase. Keep only the information needed to verify the purchase.
3. Once verified, we will reply with a **Telegram group invitation link**. Follow the instructions in the reply to join.

If you cannot use Telegram or prefer not to join, technical support remains available at the same email address.

```text
When asking for help in the group, include:
TrueODS and UE versions:
GPU and VRAM:
Relevant output settings:
Problem description and reproduction steps:
```

Do not post identity documents, phone numbers, Epic passwords, verification codes or payment-card data in the group. Redact unrelated personal and payment information from purchase evidence and send it only to the email address above, never to the group. Joining the support group does not replace a licence or add seats.

### Group announcement

Welcome to the TRUEODS Telegram support group. This group is for installation and usage questions, release notices and optional artwork sharing. The technical-support address is **trueodssupport@gmail.com**. Send logs, original frames and projects by private email; do not post them in the group chat.

Include the plugin and UE versions, GPU, output settings and reproduction steps when reporting a problem. We follow up on technical issues using reproducible reports. A chat message does not automatically open a support case or commit us to a fix date. Release notes are authoritative for shipped releases and known limitations; experimental ideas discussed in the group are not feature commitments.

### Group rules

1. Be respectful and stay on topic. No harassment, flooding or unsolicited advertising.
2. Keep logs, render diagnostics, receipts, private paths, client imagery and projects out of group chat. Review screenshots for sensitive material too.
3. Do not distribute plugin packages, accounts, keys or unauthorised third-party assets. Do not request or distribute licence-bypass or evaluation-mark-removal modifications. Applicable licence terms still govern use.
4. Share only work you have permission to show. Attribution and reuse are the author's choice. Administrators may not use work shared in the group for marketing without separate explicit permission.
5. Before reporting a problem, read the [Quick Start](QUICKSTART.md) and [FAQ](FAQ.md), then email support privately using the [email template](SUPPORT.md). Keep each case in one email thread when possible, and reply to the original email when sending more material.
6. A nickname, purchase screenshot or group membership does not establish official identity. Use only the official profile and email listed here. Never give verification codes or send money to someone merely claiming to be an administrator.
7. Administrators may hide or remove content involving private information, harassment or unauthorised distribution and remind the poster; repeated violations may lead to posting restrictions or removal from the group.

The support group is not a dedicated consultant, a 24/7 help desk, a refund authority or a way to grant licence exceptions. Membership is not required to contact email support.
